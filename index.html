How it works

Receipt in, per-person totals out.

The photo is read and audited in the seconds after you take it. Then come the five steps the app numbers, and the first is just checking Tabber got it right.

Capture

Photograph the receipt with its total in the gray band above the shutter, or pick one from Photos. The original capture is read, not a compressed copy, so small print survives.

Parse

The photo is straightened and cropped, then read as a table, so each item stays attached to its own price instead of being paired by position.

Check

The printed subtotal and total audit the parse: the items have to add up. The strip in the band is read on its own as a second opinion on the total. Where it doesn't add up, Tabber says so.

  1. Review

    Every line, and the restaurant name, is editable. Anything the audit couldn't reconcile is flagged for a glance-check before you go on.

  2. Tip

    Keep the tip the receipt already printed, pick a percentage, type an amount, or leave none. Your usual percentage, and whether it's figured pre- or post-tax, come from Settings.

  3. Seat

    Who's at the table. People Tabber already knows are one tap each, a new name is typed once, and the whole table can be said in a breath. Tap a seated name to mark their birthday.

  4. Assign

    Type or dictate who had what, and items land under each person as they're recognised. Or tap a row to place it, and hold one to place several at once — which is the whole step without Apple Intelligence.

  5. Share

    Per-person totals with itemised breakdowns, sent through the standard share sheet. Finish files the tab on your home screen.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

This isn't a policy position, it's how the app is built. Tabber has no backend to send anything to. The receipt photo, the text read off it, the names at your table, and the sentence describing who had what are all processed on the device using Apple's on-device models.

0network calls

The core flow works in airplane mode.

0accounts or passwords

No sign-ins — there is nothing to create.

0trackers or ads

No analytics, no third-party SDKs.

0data collected

As declared by the developer in the App Store privacy manifest.

You hold the delete key

Receipt photos can be kept with each tab, dropped 30 days after the meal, or not saved with tabs at all. Settings shows exactly what the photos cost on disk and can delete every one of them while leaving your tabs, itemisations, and remembered people intact. One confirmed tap erases everything.

The math

Fair by construction.

A money app has to be right every time, so none of the arithmetic is left to a language model.

Prorated, never flat

Tax and tip follow the order

Both are split by each person's share of the subtotal. Someone who had a salad doesn't subsidise the steak — and the person who ordered the steak isn't overcharged either.

Integer cents

The column always adds up

Every amount is whole cents end to end. Rounding drift lands on the largest payer, so the per-person figures always sum to exactly the total printed on the receipt.

Deterministic

The model never does math

The AI helps read the receipt and works out who had what. Every calculation after that is ordinary, testable code — the part that has to be exact is the part that never guesses.

Shared items

Split evenly or by portion

An appetiser three ways is one shared line spanning three people, not three duplicates. Uneven splits are supported when someone had rather more of it than the others.

Audited

Checked against the receipt

Items must reconcile with the printed subtotal, and the subtotal plus tax and tip must reach the printed total. A line that isn't really on the receipt gets caught this way.

Legible in a dim room

Built for the table

Tabular figures, clear hierarchy, one-handed and fast — this is a utility you reach for mid-dinner, not something to sit and study.

Requirements

What you need.

Everything you need

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later

Scan the receipt and tap who had what. The prices, tax and tip come straight off the receipt, items keep the names it prints, and the split math is identical on every iPhone.

With Apple Intelligence

iPhone 15 Pro or newer

Describe who had what in a sentence, typed or said, and item names are tidied up. Prefer the faster scan? Switch it off for Tabber in Settings.

Not required

An internet connection

The core flow makes no network calls at all. Tabber works in a basement restaurant with no signal exactly as well as anywhere else.
